Cubic

STORMHACKS 2026 BEST DESIGN AWARD

A two-player co-op puzzle game in the browser. One player walks the outside of a cube, the other is trapped inside, and neither can see the other's screen. You solve all six faces by talking through the walls.

MY ROLEFull stack, visual design
TEAM4 people
BUILT IN24 hours
Hardest problem

Keeping both players oriented as they walk across cube edges. Every face has its own "up," and the inside view is a mirror of the outside, so one shared cube-math library runs on both the client and the server to keep them in agreement.

TypeScript · Phaser 4 · Node.js · Socket.io · WebRTC · Gemini API · Vercel · Render
